Notes on App IDs

App IDs are global constants stored on Roku's servers — not device-specific. They are the same across all Roku devices worldwide.

To get IDs for any app installed on your Roku (including ones not listed here), send:
GET http://[roku-ip]:8060/query/apps
This returns an XML list of every installed channel with its numeric ID.

IDs marked "use /query/apps to verify" — Max and Apple TV+ IDs have varied across device generations. Confirm against your device's live response.

Keyboard input — send individual characters via: keypress/Lit_[char] (e.g. Lit_a, Lit_3). URL-encode for non-ASCII. Use Backspace + Enter for field control.
